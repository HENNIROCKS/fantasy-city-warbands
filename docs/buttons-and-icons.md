# Buttons and icons

What the small buttons do.

## On the start page

Tap a warband to open its cards – or the builder, while it has no fighters
yet. Beside each warband:

- ![Favourite](icons/star.svg) **Favourite** keeps it at the top of the list.
  One warband at a time.
- ![Edit](icons/edit.svg) **Edit** opens it in the builder.
- ![Roster PDF](icons/roster.svg) **Roster PDF** makes its roster, to print or
  share.
- ![Export](icons/export.svg) **Export** saves it as a file.
- ![Delete](icons/delete.svg) **Delete** removes it, after a question that
  offers the export first.

## In the builder

Beside each fighter's type and cost:

- ![Duplicate](icons/duplicate.svg) **Duplicate** recruits another of its
  kind, with the same wargear and choice, at its full price. Name it yourself.
- ![Remove](icons/delete.svg) **Remove** takes it out again. After a battle a
  fighter is paid for, and is dismissed on the back of its card instead.

Where one would go too far, the button says why in place of its icon.

## At the top

- ![Settings](icons/settings.svg) **Settings** – the settings, these guides
  and what the app is.
- ![Close](icons/close.svg) **Close** leaves Settings for the start page.
- **←** goes back a page.

## Under the cards

- ![Turn over](icons/turn-over.svg) **Turn over** shows the back of the card
  on top, to write on it. **Done** keeps what you wrote, **Cancel** drops it.
- **−** and **+** take a point of health off a fighter, or give it back.
- **State** marks a fighter, such as activated.
- **Battle**, under the warband card, starts the battle, moves to the next
  round and ends it, and shows your opponent the warband.
