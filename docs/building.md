# Building a warband

**New warband** on the start page walks you through five steps: game, faction,
the choice the whole warband makes, fighters, finish. You can come back to any
step later.

**The ring beside a game** shows roughly how much of it the app carries: each
piece is one area of the game, filled once it is built.

**Where the rules are.** At the foot of each faction a link leads to where
its rules are written: its page on wyrdcry.net, the Black Death City site, or
the PDF of a homebrew faction.

**A button is greyed out.** Tapping it would take the warband too far – over
its budget, past a fighter's limit, beyond what a fighter may carry. The button
says which.

**Something is still missing.** A leader, enough henchmen, an open choice: the
app never blocks these. It lists them on every step and again at the end.

**The warband's choice.** What it changes is counted in: a fighter it rules
out is greyed out, a characteristic it raises is in the fighter's figures,
and a fighter it makes cheaper is recruited at that price. Once the warband
has fought, the choice stays as it was picked.

**Equipment.** Each fighter gets a checklist of what its faction allows. What a
fighter can never take is named under the list. What its current gear rules
out stays in the list, greyed out with the reason. Tap a piece's name to see
what it does – a weapon's profile and rules, an item's text – and tick the box
to buy it. Where a fighter may carry two of a piece, a button under it adds
the second.

**Starting experience.** In Black Death City a fighter is recruited with
experience such as 40+d6. Roll the die at the table and tap the result under
the fighter; until then it is still to do.

**Another of the same.** Duplicate beside a fighter recruits another of its
kind with the same equipment and choice, at its full price. Give it a name of
its own.
