# The aftermath

**End battle…** opens a few pages over the deck. Roll the dice at the table
and tap what they show. Nothing is saved until you tap **Apply** – **Keep
playing** takes you back to the battle.

1. **The battle.** Pick win, draw or loss. Leave *Add to the battle history*
   on to write the battle down.
2. **Out of action.** Check who was taken out. For each of them, roll on the
   injury table and tap the result. Some results ask one more question, such
   as the surgeon. A fighter with a magic item also rolls for it: switch on
   **Stolen on a 4+** if your opponent took it.
3. **A new leader.** If your leader died, pick the hero who takes the lead.
4. **Items used.** The single-use items you ticked on the cards are already
   switched on. Switch on any you forgot to tick.
5. **Renown.** Each fighter rolls. Switch on what adds to the roll, and the
   page says which score it needs. Switch on each roll that reached it.
6. **Explore District.** Pick up to five fighters for the search party. The
   page lists what helps the roll, such as a Wyrdstone Pendulum one of them
   carries; switch on an Ornate Key you used, and it leaves the stash. Roll the
   exploration dice. If they show an encounter, tap it, and tap the answer
   where it asks one. With a Scout in the party you can tap two. If the
   encounter falls on one fighter, tap who. For the Wyrdstone Cluster, tap how
   many dice show each result. For each roll on a loot table, tap the result.
   Some finds ask for one more roll, such as the gold in a Sack of Teeth. A
   magic item you already have cannot be tapped: roll again. An encounter that
   sends a fighter to the injury table adds a page for that roll after this
   one, and a leader lost there one for the new leader. For a Lone Survivor,
   tap the henchman who joins: free, with a Move of 3. What an encounter leaves
   for the next battle, such as the Mad Prophet's Bravery or a free hired
   sword, stands on the warband card until the next aftermath.
7. **Income.** Count the shards you delivered. The page shows what the search
   party found and the favour and gold the battle brings. Hired swords show
   their fees here. A roll you have not tapped yet is listed.

**Apply** writes it all into the warband: gold, favour, injuries, the dead,
the finds in the stash, the battle history. If a fighter has a level to spend, the app asks right away.
You can also spend it later from the **Battle** menu.

A page only appears when it has something to ask.
