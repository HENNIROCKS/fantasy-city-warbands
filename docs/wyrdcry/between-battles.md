# Between battles

**Recruit and buy in the builder.** Tap the warband in the list. Add fighters
and give them equipment, as when you first built the warband. The
Trading Post is in the same list. Before you buy a rare piece, the app asks
which heroes searched for it and whether they found it.

**What you had is paid.** Once your warband has fought, the builder no longer
takes back what it had when you opened it. You can only undo what you bought
in the same visit.

**The back of a fighter's card.** Tap the pencil under the card. There you can:

- write a name, fluff and notes, and add a photo of the model
- move equipment to the stash and back
- correct renown, injuries and why a fighter sits out
- spend renown
- dismiss the fighter. Nothing is paid back.

**The back of the warband card.** Name, colour, fluff and notes. You can also
set the favour, add gold, sell or throw away what lies in the stash, and add or
strike battles.

Nothing on a back is saved until **Done**. **Cancel** drops it.

**No hero left.** If your leader is gone and no hero can take over, the
warband is disbanded. It stays in the list to look at, and the builder offers
a new warband of the same faction. Was it a mistake? The back of the warband
card takes it back.
