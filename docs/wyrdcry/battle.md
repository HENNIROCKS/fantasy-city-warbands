# The battle

The cards count the battle for you. You still roll the dice and move the models.

**Start.** Tap **Battle → Start battle** under the warband card. Your first
tap on a fighter starts it too.

**Damage.** Under a fighter's card, **−** takes one point of health and
**+** gives it back. At none the fighter is out of action, and you hear a
scream. You can switch the scream off under the gear, in Settings.

**State.** **State** marks a fighter as activated or waiting, in cover,
panicked or with its heroic reaction used. The card shows every mark,
and so do the strokes above the deck.

**Rounds.** The warband card shows the round, who still has to act and who is
out. Tap **Battle → Next round** when the round ends. Tapped by mistake? **Back
to round …** undoes it, until you tap something new.

**Morale.** The app tells you when your warband wavers. In the next
round each card asks for its Bravery test, and a failed test is marked
*Panicked*. A Plague Novice, or a Goblin among the Greenskin Marauders, counts
as half a fighter out of action, as their faction rules say.

**Zeal.** Witch Hunters count their Zeal with **−** and **+** under the warband
card. The cards add what it gives.

**Stopping.** The battle stays on your phone, even if you close the app.
**End battle…** opens the aftermath. **Cancel battle…** drops the battle, and
nothing in the warband changes.

While a battle runs, no equipment moves on the backs of the cards.
