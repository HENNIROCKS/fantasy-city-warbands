# From the Warband Deck or the Builder

You can bring a warband over from the Wyrdcry Warband Deck or the Warband
Builder on wyrdcry.net.

1. Export the warband there as a file.
2. Tap **Import warband** on the start page here and pick the file.
3. Read the sheet before you import. It lists what the app takes over
   differently – for example, purchases still pending in the builder count as
   bought, and custom abilities go into the warband's notes.

Renown, favour, gold, the stash, the battle history, fluff, notes and colour
come along. Experience does not: the Campaign Book counts renown only.

A hired sword on the builder's roster shows as unknown. Here you hire one for
a single battle – see [Hired swords](hired-swords.md).
