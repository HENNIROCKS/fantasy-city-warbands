# Hired swords

A hired sword fights for one battle and then leaves. You hire it while you set
up the battle.

1. Open the cards and tap **Battle** under the warband card.
2. Tap **Hired swords** to see those your faction may hire, each with its
   fee.
3. Tap one to hire it. Tap it again to send it away.

**A pick.** The Goblin Shaman comes in one of six roles, the Troll as a Stone
or a River Troll. Tap it, then tap the role: its card is named after it and
shows the talent, the weapon and the picture that come with it. To pick again,
send it away and hire it anew.

**Homebrew.** Six of them are homebrew, listed under their own heading at the
end. Greenskin Marauders with a wizard of their own cannot hire another one:
the button says *A WIZARD is on the roster*. Mercenaries cannot hire the Dark
Warrior beside an Elf Ranger, a Troll Slayer, a Warrior Priest or a Holy Relic,
and none of the three once she is hired: the button says what stands in the
way. Each heading links to where its hired swords are written: wyrdcry.net, or
the PDF of the homebrew ones.

You can hire before the battle starts and during the first round. Hiring also
starts the battle, as the first tap at the table does.

**Gold.** The list counts the gold you have left. If it is not enough, the
button says *Not enough gold*. You pay nothing yet: the fee comes off your gold
in the aftermath. Cancel the battle and you pay nothing.

**Half the fee.** If your Mercenaries picked *Well-connected*, the app halves
the dearest one for you. If a hero has the talent *Well-Connected*, roll the
dice at the table. On a 4+, switch on **rolled 4+** before you tap the hired
sword.

**Free.** If you picked *Calling in a debt* as the [underdog](underdog.md), or
your search party found *To the Rescue*, the next one you hire costs nothing.
Send it away and the next one is free instead.

**In the battle.** Its card comes after your fighters, and the warband card
lists it under *Hired swords*; tap its name to go there. It carries the fluff
its rules give it, and a picture whose ink takes your warband's colour. Count its damage and
mark it activated like any fighter. It does not count when the app checks
whether your warband wavers, and it has nothing to write on its back.
