# The underdog

Underdogs is an optional rule of the Campaign Book. Agree on it before the
campaign starts. A warband that has fallen behind gets help to catch up.

1. Open the cards and tap **Battle** under the warband card.
2. Tap **Underdog** and type in your opponent's reputation. The sheet shows
   your own.
3. If you are 5 or more behind, you are the underdog. You pick one bonus for
   each 5. Tap a bonus to pick it, and tap it again to put it back.

You can do this before the battle starts and during the first round, before
you hire. Being the underdog also starts the battle, as the first tap at the
table does. If you type a smaller figure later, the bonuses picked last go
first. If *Calling in a debt* goes, the hired sword it brought leaves with it.
Empty the field to take it all back.

**What the app does for you.** *Calling in a debt* makes the next hired sword
free. *Bounty* counts your income one standing higher in the aftermath. Each
renown roll needs one less.

**What you do at the table.** *Informants* and *Saboteurs* stand on the
warband card while the battle runs, so you remember them.

Each bonus can be picked once.
