# Building a gathering

Pick a Covenant as the faction, or Wretched Undead if you forsake them. A
Covenant limits the models you may recruit: the others are not offered.

**The leader.** A gathering has one. Its choices run in this order: the
element, the mana points, the spells and the Basic Leader Trait.

**Blood, Bone or Plasm.** Every model picks one, and its characteristics
change with it. A Covenant that allows only one gives it to the model, with no
choice. A Plasm leader, Husk or Gigantic Horror cannot wear heavy armour, and the button says so.

**Mana points.** The leader divides its mana points between the three schools.
Each row has a minus and a plus: the plus is greyed out once every point is
given, and the leader stays open until all of them are. Sorcerous Talent adds
one point.

**Spells.** Summon is given. The others are picked from the schools, each under
its own heading; a Recusant Maleficar's leader may pick only from necromancy at
first. Studious adds one spell.

**Traits.** A leader picks one Basic Leader Trait. Beyond that, a Covenant lets
one model of the gathering have its own trait or one from the Wretched Undead
list, and Wretched Undead give two traits to any models. The leader gets its
Covenant's trait without a pick. A trait that is used up, or that needs a
keyword the model lacks, is greyed out and says why: *None left*, *Skitter needs
Blood or Bone*. A model cannot change its element while a trait it has needs the one
it has.

**Obols.** A gathering starts with 300. Miserly adds 50, and the header shows
the new sum. The Deployment Limit of 350 stands on the warband's card and on the
roster; the app does not count the models you field yet.

**Equipment.** Each model gets a checklist of what its entry allows, with the
rules for the slots – weapons, armour, a shield, one piece of Miscellaneous
Equipment – said on the piece that would break them.
