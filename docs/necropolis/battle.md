# The battle

The cards count the battle for you. You still roll the dice and move the models.

**Start.** Tap **Battle → Start battle** under the gathering's card. Your first
tap on a model starts it too.

**Who is fielded.** A gathering may field models up to the Deployment Limit of
350 obols. Before the first round, open a model's card, tap **State** and tick
**Not fielded** for every model you leave at home. The box on the gathering's
card shows what is fielded against the limit, for example *Fielded 330 / 350*.
A model that is not fielded has no activation to make, and the count of models
still to act leaves it out.

Taking the tick away puts the model back. If that would take the gathering over
the limit, the button is greyed out and says *Over the Deployment Limit of 350*.
A gathering that is over the limit before you mark anybody shows it in the box
at once.

**Hit points.** Under a model's card, **−** takes one hit point and **+** gives
it back. At none the model is Shattered and out of action. The cards do not keep
the mana pool, the others step or the scenario; use dice and paper for those.

**End.** The battle runs until you end it. The rulebook assumes five turns,
unless you and your opponent agree on another number.
