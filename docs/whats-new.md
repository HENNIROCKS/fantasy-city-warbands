# What’s new

What changed in the app, newest first. Only what you notice at the table –
a cost, a rule, a new button. What is still to come is on the
[roadmap](https://github.com/HENNIROCKS/fantasy-city-warbands/blob/main/ROADMAP.md).

## 12 Nov 2026

- **Necropolis**
  - You can leave a model out of the battle: tick **Not fielded** under **State**. The warband card shows what is fielded against the Deployment Limit, and a model that would take you over it cannot be put back.
- **Wyrdcry**
  - Freelance Knight: Move 4 (was 5).
  - Warlock: any faction can hire him, including Elf Rangers, the Beastmen Raiders and the Shadow Warriors.

## 10 Nov 2026

- **Necropolis**
  - You can build a gathering. Pick a Covenant, recruit leaders and minions, give them armaments, an element, spells, mana and traits.
  - The card shows the Deployment Limit of 350 obols.
  - Miserly adds 50 obols to the purse only once the gathering has fought. Take it back before the first battle and the obols go with it.
- **Wyrdcry**
  - A fighter can take any number of heroic talents from its two groups. The limit of five is gone.
  - A second Hardened, Hatred or Horrible Scars stays what it is instead of turning into a Flesh Wound.
  - A Sack of Teeth goes into the stash. Sell it for d3×10 gold coins, instead of getting the gold when you find it.
  - The aftermath’s income shows only what the warband brings in.

## 8 Nov 2026

- **Black Death City**
  - The card says a piece of wargear is already included only where it changes a figure.
- **Necropolis**
  - On the start screen and ready to pick as a game.

## 6 Nov 2026

- **Black Death City**
  - Follows the Playtest v1.10.
  - Halberds and spears are Polearms. A fighter can carry a shield with one, which then makes the weapon Unwieldy.
  - The Long Bow shoots 28" (was 24") and does d3/d6 damage (was 1/d6).
  - The campaign. After a battle, the aftermath handles injuries, Black Rot, experience and a new leader. Between battles: holdings and the stronghold, Explore the City, income, curiosities and relics to sell, advancement rolls and retiring a fighter on the back of the card.
  - The Blighted Cloister: The Rot-Colossal has Demonic Strength.
  - Death Covens: New Manservant, 45 silver, up to three.
  - Death Covens: Skeleton Warriors have Bow 4 (was 6) and can take a Long Bow, a Short Bow or the Ancient Banner.
  - Death Covens: The Ogre Great Weapon is no longer Unwieldy.
  - Death Covens: The stronghold is the Woodland Estate.
  - Guild Mercenaries: The Noble costs 150 silver (was 120). He comes with a Noble’s Rapier and Seasoned Warrior instead of Trained Duellist.
  - Guild Mercenaries: Only one Plague Doctor per warband.
  - Verminkin: The Ensorcelled Halberd costs 48 silver (was 52).

## 4 Nov 2026

- **Black Death City**
  - In Street of Death the Defender takes no flee test.
  - The warband card names the money Silver.
- **Wyrdcry**
  - Two playtest factions from wyrdcry.net, the Beastmen Raiders and the Elf Rangers, under their own heading.
  - Importing from the Warband Builder brings the fighters’ talents along: magical abilities, mutations, heroic talents and injuries. What finds no place goes into the notes.

## 1 Nov 2026

- **Wyrdcry**
  - A Vampire can spend renown on the Blood of Von Carstein talents.
  - The Great Weapon costs 45 gold (was 40) and does 3/5 damage (was 3/4).
  - Possessed: Mutated weapons take up a hand and rule out ranged weapons.
  - Possessed: The Scorpion Tail is an off-hand weapon at 2/4 damage (was 3/6).
  - Undead: The Dire Wolf is a BEAST.
