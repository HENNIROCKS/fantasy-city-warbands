# The aftermath

**End battle** opens a few pages over the deck. Roll the dice at the table
and tap what they show. Nothing is saved until you tap **Apply** – **Keep
playing** takes you back to the battle.

1. **The battle.** Pick win, draw or loss. Leave *Add to the battle history*
   on to write the battle down.
2. **Holdings.** The winner rolls for the prize holding; tap it. Then take
   it, seize one of your opponent's holdings, or stand your ground. If you
   lost, tap the holdings your opponent seized. With more than five, pick
   which to give up. When the battle raises your stronghold a level, pick
   its upgrade.
3. **Out of action.** Check who was taken out. For each of them, roll on the
   injury table and tap the result. Beasts, skeletons, thralls and demons get
   their own small table. An injury asks whether it is permanent or
   temporary: roll once more and tap the answer.
4. **A new leader.** If your leader was killed, pick the champion who assumes
   command. It leads until you recruit a new leader.
5. **Experience.** Count each fighter's experience at the table – for
   surviving, for losing, for each enemy taken down, and any bonus – and enter
   the total. The page shows the rank it reaches. Heroic Escape adds its
   bonus by itself.
6. **Explore the City.** Pick the scouting party: a leader or champion, one
   to three henchmen and a beast if you like. Tap what each exploration roll
   ran into, and then what it asks for: silver, a curiosity, a relic, a
   holding, Black Rot or an injury roll for a fighter.
7. **Income.** Roll for your stronghold, each holding and the treasure you
   claimed, and enter the silver. A holding that runs dry or is ransacked
   asks for its roll here.
8. **Recuperate.** Roll for each temporary injury a fighter already had.
   Switch on the ones that heal.
9. **Downtime.** Leech a fighter's Black Rot with a Jar of Canal Leeches and
   tap whether the roll took a level.

**Apply** writes it all into the warband: the injuries, Black Rot, the
experience, the dead, the holdings, the silver, the finds in the stash and the
battle history. A fighter that is killed leaves the roster with its
equipment. Black Rot stands on the fighter's card; its fourth level kills the
fighter.

A page only appears when it has something to ask. Where an encounter does
something the app does not keep, it says so: note that on the back of the
card.

**A new rank** brings an advancement roll. Spend it on the back of the card,
as [between battles](between-battles.md) explains.
