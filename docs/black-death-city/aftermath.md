# The aftermath

**End battle** opens a few pages over the deck. Roll the dice at the table
and tap what they show. Nothing is saved until you tap **Apply** – **Keep
playing** takes you back to the battle.

1. **The battle.** Pick win, draw or loss. Leave *Add to the battle history*
   on to write the battle down.
2. **Out of action.** Check who was taken out. For each of them, roll on the
   injury table and tap the result. Beasts, skeletons, thralls and demons get
   their own small table. An injury asks whether it is permanent or
   temporary: roll once more and tap the answer.
3. **A new leader.** If your leader was killed, pick the champion who assumes
   command. It leads until you recruit a new leader.
4. **Experience.** Count each fighter's experience at the table – for
   surviving, for losing, for each enemy taken down, and any bonus – and enter
   the total. The page shows the rank it reaches. Heroic Escape adds its
   bonus by itself.
5. **Recuperate.** Roll for each temporary injury a fighter already had.
   Switch on the ones that heal.

**Apply** writes it all into the warband: the injuries, Black Rot, the
experience, the dead and the battle history. A fighter that is killed leaves
the roster with its equipment. Black Rot stands on the fighter's card; its
fourth level kills the fighter.

A page only appears when it has something to ask. Advancement rolls for a new
rank, holdings, exploring and income are not in the app yet – note them on
the back of the card.
