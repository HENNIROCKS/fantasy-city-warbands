# Black Death City

How a game night runs with the app:

1. **The battle** – pick the scenario, then count damage and rounds [on the cards](battle.md).
2. **After the battle** – the [aftermath](aftermath.md) asks for injuries and
   experience and writes them into the warband.
3. **Until the next one** – recruit, buy and write on your cards [between battles](between-battles.md).
