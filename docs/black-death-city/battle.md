# The battle

The cards count the battle for you. You still roll the dice and move the models.

**Start.** Tap **Battle → Start battle** under the warband card. Your first
tap on a fighter starts it too.

**Before the battle.** Tap **Battle → Before the battle** and enter your
opponent's rating: the app says who is the underdog, and asks who won the
roll-off when both ratings are equal. Then tap the scenario and the twist the
dice show. All three stand in a box on the warband card; tap an entry to read
its rules. You can change them until the second round.

**Prepared spells.** Before the battle each wizard and priest rolls a dice on
each lore it knows. Tap **Battle → Prepared spells** and tap what the dice
shows; the menu says how many are still to roll. The spell stands on the
caster's card for this battle. A roll that shows a signature spell brings
nothing. You can change a roll until the second round.

**Battle groups.** Most scenarios divide the warband into groups, and the
groups that do not start on the field wait in reserve. While the battle is being
set up, tap **Battle → Battle groups**. The app divides the fighters as evenly
as it can. Tap a fighter and then another to swap them, or tap a fighter and
then *Move here* under a group. Under *Starts on the field* tap the groups you set up;
all the others go into reserve. Fighters without a name show their type. A
fighter in reserve has a hatched stroke above the deck, says *In reserve* on
its card and no longer counts under *to act*. In Street of Death the Defender's
first group is the Hit and the fighters who go with them: move those into it by
hand, as the group sizes need not be equal there. Changing the scenario or its
twist starts the groups afresh, as does a change of underdog that puts you on
the other side of the scenario's rules.

**Reinforcements.** From the round the scenario names, **Battle →
Reinforcements** lists the fighters in reserve by group. Roll for each fighter
of the group you choose, as the scenario says, and tap what the dice show; the
fighter comes onto the field. A fighter that does not arrive stays in the list
for next round. Where a twist lets a group arrive without a roll, tap the
group.

**Damage.** Under a fighter's card, **−** takes one point of life and **+**
gives it back. With one point left the card says *Bloodied*. At none the
fighter is out of action, and you hear a scream. You can switch the scream off
under the gear, in Settings.

**State.** **State** marks a fighter as activated. The strokes above the deck
show who has acted.

**Rounds.** The warband card shows the round, who still has to act and who is
out. Tap **Battle → Next round** when the round ends. Tapped by mistake? **Back
to round …** undoes it, until you tap something new.

**Points.** Where the scenario is won on points – victory points, domination
marks, death marks – the score stands under the warband card, and the round
moves into the Battle menu. Tap the score to count your points and your
opponent's. Once a warband has enough and the score is not tied, it says
*Battle ends*, and at the end of the round the Battle menu offers only **End
battle**.

**Treasure.** In the scenarios with treasure, **State** offers *Holding
treasure* and *Escaped with treasure* – not for beasts and thralls. A fighter
taken out of action drops what it holds, and a fighter out of action can be marked with neither. A fighter marked as escaped no longer holds the treasure. The dice for dropping it, for fleeing
with it and for the cursed coin stay on the table. An escaped fighter no longer
has to act. Where three escapes end the battle, the score under the warband
card counts your escaped fighters from their cards; tap it to enter your
opponent's. At three the battle ends at once: the Battle menu offers only
**End battle**, and its sheet names how much treasure is held and how many
fighters escaped.

**Flee test.** Once five or more of your fighters are bloodied or out of
action, the warband card says *Flee test*. At the end of the round the Battle
menu asks for your roll: **2+** leads to the next round, **1** means your
warband flees and ends the battle. To flee of your own will, or when the
scenario is won, tap **End battle**. In Street of Death the Defender takes no
flee test and cannot flee; once **Before the battle** says who the underdog
is, the app asks the Defender for none.

**Stopping.** The battle stays on your phone, even if you close the app.
**End battle** opens the [aftermath](aftermath.md), which starts with the
result – win, draw or loss – for the warband's battle history. **Cancel
battle** drops the battle, and nothing in the warband changes.

While a battle runs, no equipment moves on the backs of the cards.
