# Deviations

Where the app deliberately differs from what a game prints or ships as data, or settles a contradiction in it one way. No session evens these out again. Ticked: confirmed by the rules author. Below each game, what only the author can settle and changes nothing here yet – ticked where they have answered.

## Wyrdcry

The data is the Warband Deck's hand-kept ruleset, which differs from the site repo where the deck's `DEVIATIONS.md` says so. What of that reaches this app:

- [x] Possessed: Tentacles carry Whipcrack, where the page says Whiplash
- [x] Possessed: Great Claw carries Pierce 3, a rule the weapon rules list only as 1 and 2
- [ ] Clan Eshin: a warband holds up to 15 fighters, as the page says, where the faction data says 20
- [ ] Clan Eshin: Murder-Stab is a Double for every fighter, as the page says, where the ability data keeps the old Triple
- [ ] Clan Eshin and Mercenaries: the allowances weeping-blades and wawr point at no weapon and are left out
- [ ] Unarmed: the weapon profile the site repo's weapon data carries (1", 2 attacks, 1/2), which the hand-kept weapons leave out
- [ ] Unholy Relic: rare, a Rarity roll of 6+ before it is bought, as the hand-kept items have it, where the site repo's item data has it common
- [ ] Devastated Eye: halves only the longest reach of a weapon, never below 1", and keeps where its reach starts – a bow's 3–18" becomes 3–9"
- [ ] Injuries: a second Hatred, Hardened or Terrible Scars counts as a Flesh Wound, as a repeated [Injury] does, where the rules say so only of results marked [Injury]

### Open upstream

- [x] Pierce: one rule, Pierce X, in place of Pierce 1 and Pierce 2
- [ ] Injury 34: named Alcoholic with the id amnesia, where its text "cannot make reactions" fits neither name
- [ ] Hero costs: in the 0.9 fighter data every henchman costs its statline plus power level, while a hero costs 5gc more, a leader 10gc and a wizard 10–15gc – whether that surcharge is meant

## Black Death City

Transcribed from the Playtest v1.01, where the Fighters List, the scrolls, the Armoury lists and the tables do not always agree.

- [ ] Zombie and Zombie Ogre cost 30 and 100 silver, as their scrolls say, where the Fighters List prints 35 and 95
- [ ] Wargear costs what the Armoury list says, where a table or item page prints another price: Coven Ogre Blade 42s (table 36s), Cloister Blowpipe 16s (table 12s), Cloister Demonic Familiar 30s (item page 20s)
- [ ] Wargear is named as the Armoury list names it: Cursed Sword (table: Cursed Blade), Lantern/Torch (item page: Torchlight/Lantern)
- [ ] Spark-drill is a melee weapon, as the Rat-Mutant List and the weapon table have it, where the Sparkseer List sells it under Ranged Weapons
- [ ] Verminkin: the Ensorcelled Weapon the list sells for 26s has no profile, since the tables print only an Ensorcelled Halberd for 52s

### Open upstream

- [ ] Mercenary Ogre: its scroll prints the Guild Mercenary's profile with Life 4
- [ ] Blighted Cloister: Priesthood of Sorcerers names the Fortified Keep instead of the Secluded Scriptorium
- [ ] Blighted Cloister: The Anointed One asks for a "Knight-Abbot Noble" and allows Rot Walkers, which the faction does not have
- [ ] Blighted Cloister: Blessed Rabble has the same leader as Priesthood of Sorcerers
- [ ] Selling from the stash: the playtest has no campaign chapter yet, so a piece only leaves the stash thrown away
