# Deviations

Where the app deliberately differs from what a game prints or ships as data, or settles a contradiction in it one way. No session evens these out again. Ticked: confirmed by the rules author. Below each game, what only the author can settle and changes nothing here yet; an answered question leaves that list, into the one above where the app still differs from what is printed.

## Wyrdcry

The data is kept in the shape of the Warband Deck's hand-kept ruleset and follows Wyrdcry 1.0: the Rulebook 1.0.1, the Campaign Book 1.0 and the site repo's data. Where it differs from them:

- [x] Captured: the ransom named is half the fighter's own cost, without what it bought, where the rules say only "half of the fighter's recruitment cost"
- [x] Well-connected (Mercenaries' State Pride): the half fee goes to the dearest hired sword, the choice that saves the most, and is rounded up, where the rule leaves the choice to the player and does not say how it rounds
- [x] Unyielding (Mercenaries' State Pride): a hired sword taken along keeps its own Bravery, where the rule raises that of "all fighters"
- [x] Lucky Charm: common, as the Campaign Book 1.0 prints it, where the 1.0 item data has it rare
- [ ] A leader recruited under the Campaign Book 0.9, who raised a characteristic as well as taking a talent with the first level of renown, keeps both
- [x] Favour tiers: 0–5, 6–15, 16–30 and 31+ with their income, as the Campaign Book 1.0 and the site's Collect Income page print them, where the 1.0 campaign rules data still has the playtest's five tiers, so the Warband Builder shows another standing for a favour of 6–40 and offers other income above 5
- [x] Superior Blackpowder: used while presenting warbands, it removes Unreliable from all equipped weapons, as the site's item data gives it, where the Campaign Book 1.0 makes it a free action removing a "blackpowder" special rule
- [x] Injury 34: Alcoholic keeps the id amnesia, which saved warbands carry, where the site's injury table now anchors it as alcoholic
- [x] Deep Wound: "the fighter survives" on a 3+ is read as no lasting effect, and the 15gc come from the gold, which the Campaign Book calls "the warband stash"
- [x] Robbed: "all equipment carried" is read as everything the fighter bought, items included
- [x] Crossbow and Handgun: 18", as the weapon data gives them, where the Rulebook 1.0.1's example on page 13 prints the Crossbow at 1–24"
- [x] Blast: with the Rulebook 1.0's text, as the Blunderbuss and the Arch-Warlock's Stormcage Halberd carry it, where the Rulebook 1.0.1's Common Weapon Rules leave it out; the site explains it, and a faction's own PDF where a weapon has it
- [x] Bejewelled Dagger: a dagger a fighter can carry, sold for 15gc, where the Campaign Book only says it can be sold
- [x] Wyrdstone Cluster: the Health lost for each 1 stays for the rest of the campaign, where the Campaign Book does not say for how long
- [x] Great Weapon: Damage 3/5 for 45gc, as the site's weapon data gives it, where the Campaign Book 1.0 prints the Warrior Priest's at 3/4 (p. 38)
- [x] Hired swords' characteristics and fees: as the Campaign Book 1.0 prints them, where the site's fighter data has the Freelance Knight at Fight 5, the Elf Ranger at Shoot 6, the Ogre Bodyguard at Shoot 2, the Rat Ogre at Fight 6 and Shoot 2 with Sheer Brutality not Brutal, and the Necromancer and the Warlock at 60gc (pp. 32–39)
- [ ] Playtest factions and hired swords: the Beastmen Raiders and the Elf Rangers hire none, where the site does not say whom they may hire
- [ ] Playtest factions and racial limits: no characteristic of a BEASTMAN, ELF or WAR HOUND in a playtest faction can be raised with renown, as the Campaign Book 1.0 prints none for them (p. 11); the Shadow Warriors' homebrew limits for the ELF are not used

## Black Death City

Transcribed from the Playtest v1.10, where the Fighters List, the scrolls, the Armoury lists and the tables do not always agree.

- [ ] Wargear costs what the Armoury list says, where an item page prints another price: Cloister Demonic Familiar 30s (item page 20s)
- [ ] Wargear is named as the Armoury list names it: Lantern/Torch (item page: Torchlight/Lantern)
- [ ] Rotling: its Demonic Rot Strike has Rend –, as its scroll prints it, where the Cloister's melee table prints -1
- [ ] Will of the Saints casts critically on 10+, where the Lore of the Saints prints [1o+]
- [ ] Rotling: as a Beast and a Demon, it rolls on the Beast Injury Table, as the Playtest v1.03 named it, where v1.10 names both tables (p. 145)
- [ ] Selling wargear brings half its cost rounded down, as the sentence says, where the box beneath prints 50% rounded up (p. 152)
- [ ] Relics: named and numbered as their entries print them – 21–22 Ring of Roses, which the Relics table leaves out, and 46 Iron Ward, which it calls Talisman of Protection; 16 on the Curiosities table is the Broken Von Zikmund Pocketwatch, which the table calls Gilded Brass Pocket Watch (pp. 153–161)
- [ ] Relics count in the warband rating at their price, as step seven says (p. 152), where the Relics page says they do not (p. 159)
- [ ] Barrow Lord and Barrow Champion: as Skeletons, they roll on the Skeleton & Thrall Injury Table, as printed, though they earn experience as no Thrall does (p. 145)

### Open upstream

- [ ] Blighted Cloister: Blessed Rabble has the same leader as Priesthood of Sorcerers
- [ ] Blighted Cloister: Demonic Strength names the Rot-Colossal's Demonic Rot Blade, where it carries a Great Demonic Rot Blade; the app shows it as printed (Playtest v1.10)
- [ ] Death Covens: the Dire Wolf's Trackers asks for a Hunting Dog in the scouting party (Playtest v1.10)
- [ ] Street of Death, Heavy Downpour: the sentence on when the rain ends breaks off; the app shows it as printed

## Necropolis

Transcribed from the Rulebook v0.9, a draft that still carries placeholders and examples that do not add up.

- [ ] Minions carry the keyword Minion, which the rulebook uses only as a heading, so that the app can count them apart from the leader (pp. 32–35)
- [ ] Aberrant Husk: Brutal applies whatever keyword it chooses, as a rule of its own, where it stands under Plasm after "in addition they have" (p. 32)
- [ ] Improvised Weapon: shown on every model without a melee weapon, where the rulebook gives it to a model that bought no weapon at all, so a model with only a ranged weapon has it too (p. 36)
- [ ] Wardart and Throwing Axe count as Miscellaneous Equipment, as p. 41 lists them, so a model takes one beside its weapons, also a Beast or a colossal model whose entry allows only claws and fangs or colossal weapons (pp. 30, 33–36, 41)
- [ ] A shield cannot be taken beside a two-handed weapon, also by a leader whose second weapon is one-handed and could use it while switched to that one (pp. 36, 40)
- [ ] A bracket after a trait, such as Blood/Bone, reads as "at least one of these keywords", checked against the keywords the model has with its element (pp. 19–26)
- [ ] A Revenant's cap of 4 spells and 4 mana points is not checked, since a recruit reaches 3 spells and 4 mana points at most (p. 30)

### Open upstream

- [ ] Whether the Covenant's trait for the leader counts towards the 3 traits a leader may have; the app lets a leader have the Basic Leader Trait, the Covenant's trait and one more (pp. 17, 19–23)
- [ ] Example Gathering: The Mire Walker, a Lich with a Heavy Polearm, costs 15 obols, where the Lich costs 0 and the Heavy Polearm 20; the app takes the costs of pp. 29 and 37 (p. 18)
